I'm the Jean-Lou Chameau Professor Emeritus of Control and Dynamical Systems (CDS) and BioEng, Caltech, where I was from 1985–2025. My research in CDS began in 1976 with my BS thesis in EE at MIT, and consulting with Honeywell. My focus in retirement is on developing and explaining Universal Layered Architectures in theory and reality, with math from controls, dynamical systems, optimization, information, and computational complexity, inspired by Bode, Shannon, and Turing. My application motivations come from aerospace, power, chemical engineering, internet, systems biology, human physiology and medicine, and more recently neuroscience, cancer immunology, and patriarchy.
